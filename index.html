FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I know a seller is real?

Open their profile to see reviews from past buyers, then look for the Ownership checked and NID verified badges on the listing. Ask for screenshots in the deal chat. A brand new seller with no reviews deserves extra care.

Does AssetNodi hold my payment?

Not yet. Money goes directly between buyer and seller, and we are not a party to the deal. Start with a small first deal, keep proof of every payment, and only tap Complete after you have full access.

What does the NID verified badge mean?

The seller sent an NID, a live selfie holding a one-time code, and our team reviewed them by hand. One NID can verify only one account. It is a strong signal, but not a guarantee, so still check before you pay.

How do I sell an account?

Log in, tap Sell, pick the platform, add honest details and at least one photo, then publish. Buyers will message you in a private deal room. You can also verify ownership to get a badge.

What if someone tries to cheat me?

Tap Report on the listing, or email assetnodi.support@gmail.com with screenshots of the chat. We can remove the listing and the account. Never share a password or a one-time code with anyone, including us.

What can I list?

Facebook pages and groups, YouTube channels, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram, websites, and PUBG or Free Fire accounts. It must be yours. Stolen, hacked or fake accounts are not allowed.
